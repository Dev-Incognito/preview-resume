# Ghazal Bhatnagar — Executive Resume Selection & Preview Portal

An interactive web application designed for **Ghazal Bhatnagar** (7× World Record Holder, National Medalist, Fitness Professional, Consultative Sales Executive, and Creative Producer) to preview, compare, and export her bespoke 3-page A4 executive resumes.

---

## 🌟 Available Resume Editions

### ✦ Edition v2: Dark Luxury & Antique Gold (The Deepika-Inspired Aesthetic)
- **Visual Personality**: High-prestige, luxury executive dossier.
- **Color Palette**: Deep Obsidian & Charcoal (`#141414`, `#1c1c1c`, `#0a0a0a`) paired with Muted Metallic Antique Gold (`#b8860b`, `#d4a827`).
- **Typography**: Classical `Cinzel` serif display typography with clean geometric `Montserrat` body.
- **Scope**: 3 Pages A4 portrait, strictly print-calibrated.

### ✧ Edition v1: Warm Ivory & Metallic Gold (The Executive Editorial Aesthetic)
- **Visual Personality**: Prestigious academic and editorial publication.
- **Color Palette**: Warm Ivory (`#fdfbf7`), Midnight Navy (`#0f172a`), and Champagne Gold (`#c5a059`).
- **Typography**: Editorial serif headings with structured corporate body typography.
- **Scope**: 3 Pages A4 portrait, strictly print-calibrated.

---

## ⚡ Portal Capabilities

- **Interactive Switcher**: Instantly toggle between Edition v2 and Edition v1.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison**: Press `C` or click "Compare" to view both editions synchronously.
- **Zoom Engine**: Scalable from 35% up to 150%, with instant "Fit Screen" and "100% (True A4 Scale)".
- **Page Jump Navigation**: Jump directly to Page 1 (Identity), Page 2 (7× World Records Dossier), or Page 3 (Capabilities & Repertoire).
- **One-Click Print to PDF**: Press `P` or click "Print / Save PDF" to open the browser's native print engine.
- **Apple Safari & Mobile Support**: Responsive viewport optimization with touch support for iPhone, iPad, and Safari mobile.

---

## 🖨️ PDF Export Instructions

For the best high-resolution print-ready PDF:
1. Click **Print / Save PDF** (or press `P`).
2. Set **Destination**: `Save as PDF`.
3. Set **Paper size**: `A4`.
4. Set **Margins**: `None`.
5. Check **Background graphics**: `Enabled`.
6. Click **Save**.
