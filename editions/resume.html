GHAZAL BHATNAGAR

Introduction

Ghazal Bhatnagar is a multidisciplinary athlete, personal trainer, fitness coach, and content creator based in Udaipur, Rajasthan.

She is a 7-time World Record Holder, recognized officially by Guinness World Records, Limca Book of Records, and India Book of Records. She is also a two-time National Medalist in Strength Lifting and Ice Stock Sport, with multiple state and district championship medals in Powerlifting and Weightlifting.

As a personal trainer and coach, Ghazal works with clients on heavy lifting, strength building, fat loss, and custom body transformations. She brings the discipline, consistency, and work ethic of a competitive strength athlete directly into her coaching to help clients achieve real, lasting results.

As a content creator, she documents her athletic training, fitness lifestyle, and mindset, sharing practical advice and inspiring people to build physical strength and confidence.
